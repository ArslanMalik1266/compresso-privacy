Compresso Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

Compresso is an Android app that makes photos, videos and PDFs smaller. It does all of this on your phone. The app is developed and published by Arslan Malik ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

We don't collect, receive, store or sell your personal data. The app has no account, no ads and no analytics, and it doesn't have Android's internet permission, so it can't upload your files.

Your files

Compresso reads the photos, videos and PDFs you allow it to access, and creates smaller copies on your device. These files are never sent to us or to anyone else by the app.

  • Compressed copies are kept in the app's private cache so you can open them again from History. You can clear them in Settings, and uninstalling the app removes them.
  • Copies you choose to save go to a folder on your device, such as Pictures/Compresso.
  • Your originals stay untouched unless you choose to replace them, and Android asks you to confirm first.
  • By default, location and camera details are removed from compressed photos, and author details from compressed PDFs. You can change this in Settings.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your settings, saved presets and compression history, which includes details such as file names and sizes, on your device. You can delete your history in the app, and uninstalling the app removes this information from your device.

Android backup

If Android backup is turned on, Android may include the app's settings and history in your device's backup to your own Google account. This is controlled by Android and your Google account, and we can't access those backups. Compressed copies in the cache aren't included. You can manage backups in your device settings.

Permissions

  • Photos and videos: to find, show and compress the files on your device.
  • Notifications: to show the progress of long video and PDF jobs.
  • Foreground service and wake lock: to keep long jobs running if you leave the app or the screen turns off.
  • Storage, on older Android versions: to read your files and save the copies you choose to keep.
  • Network state: included by a media library the app uses. It only lets the app check connection status and doesn't give it internet access.

Sharing

The app only shares a file when you tap Share and choose where it goes. From then on, the app or service you chose handles it under its own privacy policy. Similarly, Contact us opens your email app, and if you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you send, and use them only to reply.

Keeping and deleting your data

We don't hold your files or app data, so there is nothing for us to keep or delete on our side. On your device, this information stays until you remove it:

  • To delete history and cached copies, use History and Settings in the app.
  • To delete everything the app stores privately, uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings.
  • Copies you saved to your device stay there after you uninstall, until you delete them yourself.
  • Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to reply. You can ask us to delete them at the address below.

Security

Your files are processed on your device and aren't transmitted by the app. Cached copies and app data are kept in private storage that Android protects from other apps. Files you save to shared folders can be seen by other apps you've given access to them.

Google Play

Google may collect information when you download or update the app through Google Play. This is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

Compresso isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
